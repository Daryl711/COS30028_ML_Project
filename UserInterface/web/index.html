      About the model

      The task

      Each image belongs to a crop-disease combination, such as Tomato · Early blight. The DPD-Small dataset has 55 crops, 175 diseases and 333 combinations. Training covers 323 of them. The other 10 are unseen: they never appear in training and can only be recognised by combining what the model learnt about the crop and about the disease separately.

      How a prediction is made

      The model scores the crop and the disease. Every valid combination gets the score log p(crop) + log p(disease), and the combinations are ranked by that score. Unseen combinations are therefore ranked alongside the seen ones.

      Closest training matches

      The gallery shows training images that share the predicted crop, training images of the same disease on other crops, and, for a seen combination, training images of that exact class. When the model exports an image embedding, the images are ranked by cosine similarity to the photo and the similarity is shown on each card.
